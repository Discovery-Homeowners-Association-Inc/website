Moving in is disorienting. You do not know which day the trash goes out, where
the pool passes come from, or who to ask about the fence you were planning to
put up.

The Welcome Committee exists to shorten that. Someone stops by, says hello,
answers the obvious questions, and points you at the less obvious ones.

## Help out

This is one of the easiest ways to get involved in the neighborhood. It costs an
hour here and there, it does not require a skill you have to already have, and
new neighbors remember it.

Email {{email:general}} and they will connect you with
the right person.

## Just moved in?

Start with three things:

1. **[Get approval before changing anything outside your home](/rules/architectural-control/)** — fences, sheds, doors, windows, roofing.
2. **Set up your account in the resident portal** so you can pay your assessment and pull up documents online.
3. **[Come to a board meeting](/meetings/)** — every homeowner and renter is welcome, no invitation needed.
