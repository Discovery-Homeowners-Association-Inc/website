Trash goes out Tuesday and Friday mornings. Recycling alternates depending on
which side of Stauffer Road you live on, and yard waste runs twice a month from
April to December.

The full schedule is below. If you are new, the county's lookup tool will tell
you your exact recycling week by address.

Animals are the reason this rule exists. A bag left at the curb overnight is an
