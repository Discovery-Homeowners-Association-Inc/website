title: Contact
summary: The office, the committees, and where to go for things the association does not handle.
---

The office at 8740 Stauffer Road handles assessments, forms, rentals, and most
questions about the neighborhood. It is staffed part-time — the hours are on the
right, and email is often the fastest route.

If your question is about a specific problem — a broken swing, a car in your
space, a street light out — [the reporting page](/rules/report-a-problem/) will
get you to the right number faster than the office can.
