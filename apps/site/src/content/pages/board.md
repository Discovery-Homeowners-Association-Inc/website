---
title: Board & Governance
summary: Seven neighbors, elected by the membership, who run the association in their spare time.
---

The association is governed by a board of seven directors elected by the
membership. They are not employees and they are not a management company — they
live here, and they do this on evenings and weekends.

The board meets on the third Tuesday of every month at the Recreation Center,
and **any homeowner or renter can attend**.
