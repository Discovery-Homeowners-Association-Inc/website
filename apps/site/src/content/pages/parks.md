---
title: Parks & Playgrounds
summary: Twenty parks and open spaces, inspected once a year and maintained by the association.
---

Twenty parks and open spaces are scattered through the neighborhood — the tot
lots between the townhouse courts, the open greens, and the paths connecting
them. They are association property, which means they are paid for out of your
assessment and maintained on your behalf.

## Reporting something broken

Do not wait for the annual inspection. If a swing is hanging by one chain or a
slide has a crack in it, tell the office and it gets logged and scheduled.
