---
title: About Discovery
summary: How the neighborhood came to be, and the people who keep it running.
---

Discovery is a neighborhood of single-family homes and townhouses in
Walkersville, Maryland — about 160 acres, laid out in 1972 as Frederick County's
first planned unit development.

The association owns and looks after the things no single household does: the
pool, the Recreation Center, the RV lot, and roughly twenty parks and open
spaces.
