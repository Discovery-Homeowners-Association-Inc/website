---
title: Pay your assessment
summary: What the assessment is, when it is due, and every way to pay it.
---

The assessment pays for everything the association looks after on your behalf:
the pool, the Recreation Center, the RV lot, twenty parks, trash collection,
landscaping, and street lights.

Payments are due on the first day of each quarter. Late payments are charged a
penalty each month until they are paid.

If you are having trouble paying, call the office at {{phone:office}} before the
due date. It is a much easier conversation beforehand.
