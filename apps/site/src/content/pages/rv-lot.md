title: RV Lot
summary: Storage for boats, trailers, and recreational vehicles. There is a monthly fee, and registration is enforced.
---

The association keeps a lot where residents can store boats, trailers, and
recreational vehicles instead of leaving them on the street or in a driveway.

## There is now a fee

The RV lot used to be free. It is not any more — the association began charging
for it on 1 October 2025, and the fee is what will eventually pay for repairing
the fence and marking out the spaces.

## Registration is not optional

If you have anything in the RV lot, it has to be registered with the office.

<aside class="callout callout--warning">

Anything in the lot that is not registered with the office is towed by Dorsey's
Towing, at the owner's expense. There is no warning notice first.

</aside>

Registration is straightforward — the form, a copy of the vehicle's
registration, and a photo. Ask the office for the current application, or call

{{phone:office}}.

<section lang="es">

**Solicitud de lote para vehículos recreativos**

Si tiene un barco, un remolque o cualquier otro vehículo recreativo en el lote,
debe enviar el formulario junto con el certificado de registro y una foto. Por
favor, envíenos esta información antes de la fecha límite.

Desde el 1 de octubre de 2025 el lote ya no es gratuito: la tarifa es de **$50
por mes** o **$150 por trimestre**, pagadera junto con su cuota trimestral.

Todos los vehículos en el lote que no estén registrados en la oficina serán
remolcados por Dorsey's Towing a cargo del propietario.

</section>
