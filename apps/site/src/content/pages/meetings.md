The board of directors meets on the third Tuesday of every month at the
Recreation Center.

**Any homeowner or renter can attend.** You do not need to be on an agenda, ask
permission, or have a complaint. People come to listen, and that is a perfectly
good reason to be there.

## What gets discussed

The recurring business is money and maintenance: the budget, contracts for
landscaping and trash, the pool season, park repairs, and whatever project is
currently in flight. Architectural Control matters are handled by that committee
and come to the board when they need a decision.

## Bringing something up

If you have a question or a proposal, two things make it go better. Email the
office beforehand so it can go on the agenda — {{email:general}} — and
bring the specifics: the address, the date, the photo. Boards can act on
specifics and can only nod sympathetically at generalities.
