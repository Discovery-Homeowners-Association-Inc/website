## Welcome to Discovery

Discovery is a neighborhood of single-family homes and townhouses in
Walkersville, Maryland. When ground was broken in 1972 it was the first planned
unit development in Frederick County — a deliberate mix of housing, parks, and
shared amenities on about 160 acres of former farmland.

The association exists to look after the things no single household owns: the
pool, the Recreation Center, the RV lot, and roughly twenty parks and open
spaces. It is run by a volunteer board of directors elected by the people who
live here, and it is funded by the annual assessment.

## New to the neighborhood?

Three things are worth knowing in your first week.

**Get approval before you change the outside of your home.** Fences, decks,
sheds, windows, doors, siding, roofing, solar panels — anything visible from
outside needs an Architectural Control Committee application first. It is a
short form, and it protects your investment as much as your neighbor's.
[Start here](/rules/architectural-control/).

**Set up your account in the resident portal.** You can pay your assessment,
pull up association documents, and send questions to the office without a stamp.

**Come to a board meeting.** They are held monthly and every homeowner and
renter is welcome — you do not need a reason or an invitation to attend.
