Phase 1 of the water main replacement covers Inspiration Court, both stretches of
Inspiration Avenue, Triumphant Court, Successful Way, and Curiosity Court.

If you live on one of those streets, expect construction traffic and the
occasional short interruption to your water supply. Notices go out ahead of any
planned shut-off.

The full plan-and-profile drawings are in the [document library](/documents/),
and the [projects page](/board/projects/) tracks where things stand.
