Registration for the 2026 pool season is open. Early-bird pricing — **$35 per
person** — runs until **15 April**. After that it is $45.

Children five and under swim free. You need to be current on your assessment to
buy a pool membership.

Season dates, hours, and the fee tables are on the [pool page](/amenities/pool/).
