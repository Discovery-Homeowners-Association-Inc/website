---
title: The RV lot is no longer free
date: 2025-08-11 09:00:00-04:00
summary: From 1 October 2025 the lot costs $50 a month, or $150 a quarter paid with your assessment.
category: general
---

The association now charges for the RV lot. The fee is **$50 per month per
household**, or **$150 per quarter** paid alongside your quarterly assessment.

Pay by Zelle, with **RV Lot Payment** in the memo line.

A few rules came in at the same time: one vehicle per household, current
registration on every vehicle, and keep both the vehicle and the space around it
tidy.

The fee is what will pay for repairing the fence, repainting the lines and space
numbers, and improving security. Full details are on the
[RV lot page](/amenities/rv-lot/).
